An ordinary object can start an unexpected story. Humour emerges from people's character and the consequences of action. The world has business of its own: a grand plan may fail, while a small act may change a possibility available to everyone. Causes remain real even when the player does not yet know them. The creator product helps authors create, play, understand and change such a world.
